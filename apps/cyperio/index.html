Cyperio

A fast and simple VPN app for Cyperio subscribers. Add your subscription link once, tap Connect, and your internet traffic travels through an encrypted connection to Cyperio servers in several countries.

When is Cyperio useful?

On public Wi-Fi

Networks in cafes, hotels and airports are shared with strangers. Cyperio encrypts the traffic between your phone and our servers, so others on the same network can't read it.

While traveling

Keep using the internet the way you do at home, wherever you are, with servers in several countries to choose from.

When your network is limited

Some networks slow down or block certain sites and services. Connecting through Cyperio can help you reach them, depending on your network.

For everyday privacy

Websites and apps you visit see a Cyperio server's IP address instead of yours.

Features

  • One tap to connect and disconnect
  • Add your subscription by link or QR code
  • Server list updates automatically
  • Choose which apps use the VPN
  • English, Persian and Arabic interface
  • No ads

How to start

  1. Install Cyperio from Google Play on your Android phone.
  2. Add the subscription link you received from Cyperio.
  3. Tap Connect.

A Cyperio subscription is required. Please use Cyperio in line with the laws of your country and the terms of the services you use. Cyperio is not offered in Oman.
