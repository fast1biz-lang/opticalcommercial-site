Cyperio Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

Cyperio is a VPN app and service provided by Optical Commercial International LLC, a company registered in the Sultanate of Oman ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the Cyperio app and the Cyperio service handle, why, and for how long. It adds to our company privacy policy; where the two differ, this policy applies to Cyperio.

1. How the VPN works

Cyperio uses Android's VpnService to send your device's internet traffic through an encrypted tunnel to Cyperio servers, which then send it on to its destination. You can choose which apps use the VPN. The VPN is the app's core function. We do not use it to read the content of your traffic, insert ads, redirect your traffic for ads or monetisation, or collect data for any purpose other than providing the service.

2. Data in the app

  • The app stores your subscription link, server list and settings on your device only.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • To refresh your server list, the app connects to our subscription server with your subscription link. That request contains your subscription token and the app version.
  • Logs shown inside the app stay on your device unless you choose to send them to us for support.

3. Data on our servers

  • Account data: the name or username of your account, your subscription token, plan, expiry date and data limit. If you contact us or buy a plan through a channel such as Telegram, we also keep the details of that conversation or purchase needed to provide and support your plan.
  • Data usage: the total amount of data (in bytes) your account uses each month, to apply your plan's data limit.
  • Connection logs: our servers record, for each connection, the time, your account identifier, the IP address you connect from, and the destination host and port. We do not record the content of your traffic. We use these logs only to run the service: to enforce the number of devices allowed on a plan, to stop abuse of our network, and to fix connection problems. They are deleted automatically after 7 days.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising. We share data only with hosting providers that run our servers (under obligations to protect it), and with authorities where the law requires it. Our servers are in several countries, so your data may be processed outside Oman.

5. Retention

  • Connection logs: 7 days.
  • Monthly data usage: until the end of the following month.
  • Account data: while your account is active, and up to 12 months after it ends, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

6. Your rights and deleting your data

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, including deleting your Cyperio account, by emailing admin@opticalcommercial.com with your account name. Removing the app deletes everything stored on your device. These rights apply under the laws that cover you, including Oman's Personal Data Protection Law (Royal Decree 6/2022).

7. Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted. Access to our servers and records is limited to the people who operate the service.

8. Children

Cyperio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

We will post any change on this page with a new effective date.
