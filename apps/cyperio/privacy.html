Cyperio Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

Cyperio is a VPN app provided by Optical Commercial International LLC, a company registered in the Sultanate of Oman. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

1. How the VPN works

Cyperio uses Android's VpnService to send your device's internet traffic through an encrypted connection to Cyperio servers. Providing the VPN is the app's only purpose. We do not read the content of your traffic, show or insert ads, or sell your data.

2. Data we use

  • On your device: your subscription link, server list and settings. These stay on your device.
  • Account: your account name, subscription plan and expiry date, so we can provide your plan.
  • Technical data: such as your IP address and the amount of data you use, which our servers need to connect you and to apply your plan's limits.

The app has no advertising or analytics tools.

3. Sharing

We do not sell or share your data for advertising. We share it only with the hosting providers that run our servers, or with authorities when the law requires it.

4. Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time by emailing admin@opticalcommercial.com. Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on your device.

5. Children

Cyperio is not intended for children under 13.

6. Changes and contact

We will post any change to this policy on this page. Questions: admin@opticalcommercial.com
